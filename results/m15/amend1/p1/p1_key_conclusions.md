Body weight: the weight effect is not a defect; each model runs with its own exponent (0.711 for Model 1 and 0.705 for Kovalenko 2016; typical AUC0-inf ratio at the global 5th percentile weight 1.207117 and 1.206895; weight acts only through Vc, and the exponent times the normalized bioavailability sensitivity of AUC0-inf at the global median weight (76.0 kg) is 0.711 x 1.564 = 1.112 and 0.705 x 1.574 = 1.110, so the near-equality is a coincidence). Log weight explains a share of 0.051 to 0.087 (R-squared) of the variance of log AUC0-last over models and populations; the 75 kg stratum term absorbs 73 to 74% of that share in the global and 14% in the Korean population. Stratified against unstratified analysis: no material change in either model: power at true GMR 0.95 (less favourable difference, Kovalenko 2016: 0.2 pp, 95% CI -0.3 to 0.8); median against 75 kg boundary: no material change in either model: power at true GMR 0.95 (less favourable difference, Model 1: -0.5 pp, 95% CI -2.3 to 1.3).
