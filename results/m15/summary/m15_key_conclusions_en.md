# Key conclusions (generated from the result files)

All results are conditional on a primary model that did not pass the pre-specified external check (plan section 6.2) and on the published parameter point estimates (no relative standard errors are recorded, so parameter uncertainty was not propagated).

Abbreviations: GMR, geometric mean ratio; pp, percentage points; CI, confidence interval; F, bioavailability; ka, absorption rate constant; ke, linear elimination rate constant; Vmax, maximum Michaelis-Menten elimination rate; AUC0-last, area under the concentration-time curve from time zero to the last quantifiable concentration; CV, coefficient of variation; AUC0-inf, area under the concentration-time curve from time zero to infinity; AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve.

1. The primary model did not pass the original healthy-volunteer studies under the pre-specified rule (outcome: fails under the fixed weight assumption only; detection probability of the combined rule at a 25% bias 99%).
2. At 130 randomized per arm (primary scenario) the simulated power at a true GMR of 0.95 is 95.2% and the joint consumer risk is 2.6% at 0.80 and 2.3% at 1.25 (single-endpoint maxima 5.3% and 4.9%); the design meets the pre-specified adequacy reading.
