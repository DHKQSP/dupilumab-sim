# Table 1. Target-mediated share of central elimination after a single 300 mg SC dose (A) and at steady state (B)

Plan section 17 (addendum 1, D-071). f_TM = [Vm/(Km + C)] / [Vm/(Km + C) + ke], C = central concentration. 1,000 subjects per scenario with between-subject variability (the same subjects in every scenario, weight and model), no residual error, no anti-drug antibodies. Each cell: typical subject; median (5th to 95th percentile) over subjects. Rows in % of subjects give shares of the 1,000 subjects. Model 1 = Kovalenko 2020 Table 1 Model 1 (primary model); Kovalenko 2016 = sensitivity model. Study Day = days after the (first) dose + 1.

Notes.
- Study Day 1.0 in a crossing row is the dose time: f_TM is at or above the threshold from the dose onward because Cmax does not exceed the threshold concentration. The rows for subjects whose f_TM falls below the threshold leave those subjects out. 'not reached' = f_TM still below the threshold at day 140, or the percentile includes such subjects.
- In the rows for subjects whose f_TM falls below the threshold, [n = ...] is the number of such subjects, and NA in the typical-subject position means that the typical subject does not fall below the threshold.
- The concentration at the crossing equals the threshold concentration by construction; it depends only on Vm, ke and Km, not on weight. Below the LLOQ, f_TM approaches Vm / (Vm + ke Km), which depends on the fixed Km.
- AUC0-last = model AUC from the dose to the earlier of the true Tlast at the LLOQ (0.078 mg/L) and day 56 after the dose (study Day 57), the dense-sampling truth of plan section 8.2(a). The share counts every time with f_TM at or above the threshold: the absorption phase before C first exceeds the threshold concentration and the phase after the crossing (the part accrued after Cmax is shown as a percentage of AUC0-last).
- Numbers are shown to one decimal as requested; Monte Carlo standard errors of the percentiles (subject bootstrap, 1,000 resamples) are in tm_table1.csv.
- Post hoc check (plan section 17.11; supplementary): the B interval (days 112 to 126) is close to, but not fully at, steady state; at 75 kg its AUCtau, as a percentage of the true steady-state AUCtau, has a median of 99.2% for Model 1 (5th to 95th percentile 94.3 to 100.1) and 98.8% for Kovalenko 2016 (5th to 95th percentile 90.7 to 100.1). At true steady state the median f_TM is 19.1% at Cavg and 22.1% at Ctrough (Model 1); 20.3% at Cavg and 23.7% at Ctrough (Kovalenko 2016) (tm_true_ss.csv). The registered values above are not replaced.
