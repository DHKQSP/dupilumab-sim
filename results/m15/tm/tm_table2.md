Notes.
- AUC0-last = model AUC from the dose to the earlier of the true Tlast at the LLOQ and study Day 57. In scenario B, AUC0-last and Cmax of the request are read as AUCtau,ss and Cmax,ss; Ctrough,ss is added.
- The reference columns are not study endpoints. The B reference is the AUC from the Week 16 dose to infinity with dosing stopped; it includes the washout of drug from all earlier doses, so it is not the steady-state counterpart of the single-dose AUC0-inf (that counterpart is AUCtau,ss).
- Other weights, the Monte Carlo standard error, the median individual change with its 5th and 95th percentiles, and the typical subject: tm_elasticity_full.csv.
- Post hoc check (plan section 17.11; supplementary): days 112 to 126 are close to, but not fully at, steady state. At 75 kg the registered-interval AUCtau, as a percentage of the true steady-state AUCtau (repeat doses to day 2240), has a median of 99.2% for Model 1 (5th to 95th percentile 94.3 to 100.1) and 98.8% for Kovalenko 2016 (5th to 95th percentile 90.7 to 100.1). At true steady state the geometric-mean changes of AUCtau,ss over the same subjects are, for ke x 0.8 and x 1.2: 25.0% and -16.7% (Model 1); 25.0% and -16.7% (Kovalenko 2016) (with C far above Km, AUCtau,ss = (F x Dose / Vc - Vm x tau) / ke, which gives +25.0 and -16.7); for Vm: 5.2% and -5.2% (Model 1); 5.4% and -5.5% (Kovalenko 2016); for F: -25.2% and 25.2% (Model 1); -25.5% and 25.4% (Kovalenko 2016); Cavg changes exactly as AUCtau. The registered values above are not replaced.
